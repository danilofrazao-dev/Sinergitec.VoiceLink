APPLE II HI-RES (HGR) KALEIDOSCOPE
Emulating Apple II 280x192 High-Resolution Graphics Mode
Original Applesoft BASIC HGR Listing: 
10  REM APPLE II HI-RES KALEIDOSCOPE
20  HGR : REM SWITCH TO 280X192 GRAPHICS
30  HCOLOR= 3 : REM 1=GREEN 2=VIOLET 3=WHITE 5=ORANGE 6=BLUE
40  FOR T = 0 TO 6.28 STEP 0.05
50    X1 = 140 + 100 * SIN(2 * T)
60    Y1 = 96  + 70  * COS(3 * T)
70    X2 = 140 + 100 * SIN(3 * T)
80    Y2 = 96  + 70  * COS(2 * T)
90    REM 4-QUADRANT SYMMETRIC HPLOT LINES
100   HPLOT X1, Y1 TO X2, Y2
110   HPLOT 280-X1, Y1 TO 280-X2, Y2
120   HPLOT X1, 192-Y1 TO X2, 192-Y2
130   HPLOT 280-X1, 192-Y1 TO 280-X2, 192-Y2
140 NEXT T : GOTO 40
